Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about American Express stock, dividends, and investor resources.

How do I buy American Express stock?

American Express common shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol "AXP." You can purchase shares through a registered broker-dealer.

Does American Express pay a dividend?

Yes, American Express pays a quarterly dividend. Please visit our Stock Information page for the latest dividend information.

When does American Express report earnings?

American Express typically reports quarterly earnings in January, April, July, and October. Please visit our Events page for specific dates.

How do I contact Investor Relations?

You can reach our Investor Relations team via email or phone. Please visit our Contact Information page for details.

Where can I find SEC filings?

All SEC filings are available on our Earnings & SEC Filings page. You can also find them on the SEC's EDGAR system.
